Hola, I’m Laura.

I’m a painter, illustrator and designer from Madrid, now living in Ireland. Every summer growing up was spent in Sancti Petri, Cádiz, and ever since I was 12 I knew I wanted to live in Ireland. So I made it happen.

Lately I’ve been painting almost only in acrylics. Alongside that I make digital paintings and design for clients. At Netsoc I started as First Year Rep in my first year of college, then became Graphics Designer and later Graphics Officer. I’m drawn to bold colour, flat shapes and a little bit of drama.

When I’m not painting, I’m probably adding another iced matcha to my list or playing folklore on repeat.
